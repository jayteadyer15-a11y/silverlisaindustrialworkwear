About Silver Lisa

Silver Lisa Industrial Workwear Ltd is a Trinidad and Tobago-based industrial apparel company focused on delivering high-quality workwear and protective clothing for professionals operating in demanding industries.

Our brand is built around safety, durability, comfort, and modern professional design. We are committed to building a trusted industrial workwear brand recognized for quality craftsmanship, professional standards, and long-term industry reliability.

Our Mission

Silver Lisa Industrial Workwear Ltd is committed to providing high-quality industrial workwear and protective apparel that combines safety, durability, comfort, and professional design for workers operating in demanding environments.

Our Vision

To become a leading industrial workwear brand recognized for innovation, quality, safety, and professional excellence across the Caribbean and international markets.
